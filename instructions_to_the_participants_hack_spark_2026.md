### **Hack Spark 2026**
*A National Level 24-Hours Hackathon*  
**Dates:** 8th and 9th October, 2026  

---

## Instructions to the Participants

All participating teams are requested to carefully read and follow the instructions given below throughout the hackathon.

---

### 1. Identity Card
Every team member must carry a valid **College ID Card** and produce it whenever requested by the organising team.

---

### 2. Venue & Seat Allotment
* The venue details and workstation/seat allotment will be shared through the official **Hack Spark 2026 Participant Community** in PDF format.
* Teams are requested to check the community regularly for all official announcements and updates.

---

### 3. Registration of Team
To register, participants can scan the QR code and fill out the Google Form at the allotted venues.

---

### 4. Inauguration & Hackathon Schedule
* **Inauguration Ceremony:** Begins at **9:30 AM on 8th October 2026** at *NIITM, 2nd Floor - Seminar Hall*.
* **Hackathon Start:** The 24-hour hackathon timer begins at **11:00 AM** at the respective allotted venues.
* **Late Arrivals:** Participants joining late for any reason will **not** be given extra time. Teams must be ready as per the evaluation schedule.

---

### 5. Team Presence at Workstation
* At least **50% of the registered team members** must be present at the allotted workstation at all times during the hackathon.
* Teams must ensure proper coordination and availability during all evaluation stages.

---

### 6. Monitoring & Silent Observation
Silent Observers / Monitoring Team members will move around the workstations periodically to monitor progress and ensure adherence to hackathon rules.

---

### 7. Evaluation Schedule

| Stage | Time | Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Evaluation** | 2:00 PM onwards, 8 October 2026 | Initial evaluation of participating teams. |
| **Selection Announcement** | 04:00 PM, 8 October 2026 | Teams selected for the next round will be announced and must move to their allotted venue by 4:15 PM. |
| **Second Evaluation** | 6:00 PM onwards, 8 October 2026 | Intermediate evaluation stage. |
| **Third / Final Evaluation** | 7:30 AM onwards, 9 October 2026 | Final evaluation by the jury panel. |

> **NOTE:** Teams must be available at their workstations and ready for evaluation at the scheduled times. Any delay or absence during evaluation may affect the team's assessment.

---

### 8. Hackathon Discipline
* Participants must maintain professional conduct, discipline, and decorum throughout the 24-hour event.
* Teams must work only from their allotted workstation unless otherwise permitted by the organising team.
* Participants should keep their workstations clean and organized.
* Any form of misconduct, disturbance, damage to institutional property, or violation of rules may lead to immediate disqualification.

---

### 9. Official Communication
* All important announcements, schedule changes, instructions, and updates will be communicated through the official **Hack Spark 2026 Participant Community**.
* Team members are responsible for checking the community regularly and keeping themselves updated.

---

### 10. Valedictory Ceremony
* **Time:** 10:00 AM, 9 October 2026  
* **Venue:** NIET - Nethaji Hall - Abdul Kalam Block  

*All participants are requested to attend the Valedictory Ceremony and remain present until the conclusion of the program.*

---

### 11. General Instructions
* Follow all instructions given by the Hack Spark 2026 Organising Team, Volunteers, Mentors, and Jury Members.
* Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
* Any decision taken by the Hack Spark 2026 Organising Committee regarding event conduct, evaluation, shortlisting, or disqualification shall be final.
* Participants are expected to demonstrate teamwork, innovation, professionalism, and respect throughout the event.

---

> **Hack. Innovate. Inspire.**  
> *Wishing all teams the very best for Hack Spark 2026!*
